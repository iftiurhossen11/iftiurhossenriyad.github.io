01 — About

Personal Profile

I am a dedicated Cyber Security Engineering undergraduate at the University of Frontier Technology, Bangladesh (UFTB), driven by a deep commitment to building secure, resilient, and human-centric technology ecosystems.

As the Founder & CEO of MyGenie, I merge security architecture with artificial intelligence to solve real-world accessibility and automation challenges for businesses and individuals across South Asia.

My journey spans offensive and defensive security research, full-stack application development, and technical leadership. Beyond engineering intrusion detection systems, threat-monitoring platforms, and AI knowledge tools, I hold a strong passion for creative literature — actively writing contemporary Bengali poetry, recording travel narratives, and researching the socio-political history of the Indian Subcontinent.

Overview

Bilingual AI assistant with workspace isolation, grounded business knowledge, multi-channel messaging (WhatsApp, Messenger, Email), and seamless human staff handoff.

Key Responsibilities

Product vision, security architecture, RBAC, workspace isolation, prompt grounding, and MVP deployment.

Proposed MVP Workflows

Bilingual AI chat, approved business knowledge, personal productivity, orders and bookings, human handoff, and an administrative dashboard.

Trust & Action Controls

Separate workspaces and role permissions; confirm consequential actions and hand off uncertain or sensitive conversations to a person.

MVP Roadmap

The proposal targets a 12-week MVP. Voice features and broader messaging coverage are planned after core workflows are validated in pilots.
